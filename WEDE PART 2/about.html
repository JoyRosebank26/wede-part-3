About Us

Learn about our origins, our mission, and the dedicated leaders behind the Olifantsfontein Youth Development NPC.

Our History

Founded in response to the growing need for youth empowerment in the Olifantsfontein area, our NPC has grown from a small grassroots community support group into a structured hub for educational and vocational training.

Our Mission Statement

To equip young people with the practical tools, confidence, and resources needed to overcome socioeconomic barriers and secure meaningful employment or entrepreneurial ventures.

Our Vision Statement

A self-sustaining, empowered community where every youth has access to growth opportunities, mentorship, and a secure future.
